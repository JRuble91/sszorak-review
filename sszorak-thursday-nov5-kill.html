Sszorak is dead.

Pull 18 of the night, pull 221 since Oct 15. This is everything that went right on the kill.

Pull 221. Seven nights, starting Thu, Oct 15, and this was the one. It was the cleanest Sszorak we've played: at 1:45 we'd lost 2 people (one of them already back up). Our wipes had usually lost 4 by then, and only 17 of the 82 wipes that got that far were as clean.

From there it was a damage race we won by inches. The boss went into the third winds at 8.2% and came out at about 1%. It enraged at 6:22.0, three seconds after the winds, and died 1.9 seconds later at 6:23.9.

Nobody carried this alone. Tornado hits were down to a third of a normal pull, every Fury pop was soaked without a single death after it, the healers had a cooldown on every pop, and we got four rezzes off mid-fight.

Survival

We stayed alive through the first two cycles

Every wipe on this boss started the same way: people died early and the raid ran out of soakers. This time the first death was Nikani at 0:55, and she was back on her feet 2.2 seconds later with Reincarnation. The first death that stuck was Cøwtrippen to Venomous Surge at 1:31.

Tornadoes

We dodged the tornadoes

Tempest went off 6 times and the raid took 19 hits in total: 3.2 per cast. Our wipes averaged 9.8 per cast, and Thursday's own wipes 9.8. Wipefest ranks the kill's Tempest score 93 out of 100 against other raids.

11 of 20 took no tornado hits at all: Aeryx, Balvos, Bargirl, Cøwtrippen, Dishwasher, Furcus, Gladrix, Mìssmurder, Prevokdsaint, Soulrending, Yanessa.

Only one death on the kill came from a tornado (Yanessa at 4:51), and Gladrix had her back up in 3.6 seconds.

On Tuesday we flagged the fourth Fury pop (~3:20) as the one with nothing raid-wide available. On the kill, Elevenzees put Divine Hymn down at 3:19, one second before it. Hymn ran on its 2-minute rotation: 1:00 on the Ravage/Mutilate stack, 3:19 for the fourth pop, and 5:20 going into the sixth.

Rezzes

Four rezzes kept the raid at strength

  • Nikani died at 0:55 (no killing blow logged). Nikani brought herself back with Reincarnation 2.2 s later.
  • Cøwtrippen died at 1:31 (Venomous Surge). Aroarian brought them back with Raise Ally 38.4 s later.
  • Cøwtrippen died at 3:38 (Venomous Surge). Aroarian brought them back with Raise Ally 76.0 s later.
  • Yanessa died at 4:51 (Tempest). Gladrix brought them back with Intercession 3.6 s later.

Aroarian's two Raise Allys both went on Cøwtrippen. Gladrix's Intercession on Yanessa came 3.6 s after she died, so we were never a healer down for long.

Defensives

People pressed buttons to stay alive

29 healthstones and health potions went out, used by 14 of 20 players. The DPS and healers pressed 81 personal defensives between them. The most: Bargirl 11, Aroarian 8, Furcus 7, Balvos 6.

Externals went where they were needed: Guardian Spirit on Nikani (0:47) and Prevokdsaint (4:47), Blessing of Sacrifice on Mangol and Nikani, Lay on Hands on Aroarian at 1:15, and Ironbark on Kegales at 3:30.

Damage

Damage held up to the very end

Lust went out at the pull and the boss was at 68.5% when the first winds started. At 4:44, where our best wipe ended (Nov 3 pull 39, 27.8%), the boss was at about 21% on the kill, with 18 of us alive.

10 players saved their second combat potion for the last 80 seconds. The boss went into the third winds with 8.2% left and lost 7.2% during those winds alone, which left about 1% for the final push.

Top DPS by parse: Çhris 75, Aroarian 73, Dishwasher 73, Bargirl 65, Furcus 60. Both tanks parsed well too: Soulrending 87, Kegales 66. Raid DPS over the whole fight: 2.75 million.

Mechanics

Crosswinds, Cysts and Virulence didn't get us

Viscous Cyst pops ended 15 of 39 pulls on Tuesday. On the kill, nobody died to a Cyst. Nobody died to Virulence either, and Wipefest scores our Virulence damage 100. Crosswinds killed 1 person (Bungieseeds at 2:54).
